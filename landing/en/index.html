How It Works

No complicated setup. Just three steps, and your practice log starts building up.

STEP 01

Log Today's Conditions

Just pick the wave size, wind direction, and board you're using. Tap "GO SURF!" to log the session.

STEP 02

Set Your Focus

Create your own "Focus Tasks" — takeoff, bottom turns, paddling, anything you're working on. Jot down tips the moment they click.

STEP 03

Review & Improve

Check your session satisfaction and per-task scores on the dashboard. See exactly where your practice time goes — and how you're progressing.
